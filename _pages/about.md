Hi! I am an Computer Vision Software Engineer at [Samsung Research](https://research.samsung.com/){:target="_blank"} in Seoul, working in the Reality Media Lab. My work revolves around building on-device computational imaging systems, 3D reconstruction, and spatial perception algorithms for edge and XR devices.

Previously, I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from the [Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur](https://www.iitk.ac.in/){:target="_blank"} in 2024, where I  earned the Ratan Swarup Memorial Prize as the Best All-Round Graduating Student. During my undergraduate studies, I also gained early research experience under [Prof. Ashutosh Modi](https://ashutosh-modi.github.io/) in Affective Computing. I also worked with [Prof. Priyanka Bagade](https://iitk.ac.in/priyanka-bagade) on the [Jal Jeevan Mission](https://jaljeevanmission.gov.in/) government project for water conservation through improved water leakage detection. 

My general research and engineering interests lie in generative modeling and 3D perception under compute and/or time constraints. Over the years, I have explored various areas including 3D Gaussian Splatting, visual-inertial SLAM, multi-object tracking, 3D human motion synthesis with diffusion models, real-time motion blur generation, and full-frame 6DoF video stabilization.
